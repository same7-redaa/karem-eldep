International Arbitration & Foreign Investors Lawyer in Egypt 2026: CRCICA Representation & Award Enforcement

محامي تحكيم دوليمركز القاهرة CRCICAاتفاقية نيويورك 1958منازعات استثمار أجنبيتنفيذ أحكام التحكيم بمصر

1. Why Foreign Investors Rely on Commercial Arbitration in Egypt

International arbitration offers complete confidentiality, relatively swift adjudication compared with ordinary litigation, party autonomy in selecting specialist arbitrators, and freedom of choice of governing law.

2. Statutory Framework: Egyptian Arbitration Law No. 27 of 1994

Derived from the UNCITRAL Model Law, establishing the separability of the arbitration agreement, kompetenz-kompetenz, and finality of arbitral awards.

3. Legal Representation Before CRCICA

Comprehensive representation before the Cairo Regional Centre for International Commercial Arbitration, interim emergency relief, and multilingual hearings.

4. Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards Under the 1958 New York Convention

Filing the exequatur petition before the Court of Appeal, obtaining immediate writ of execution, and levying judicial attachment on debtor assets in Egypt.
