Detailed Legal Services Scope in Greater Cairo

Companies & Investment

As the main government headquarters are located in the capital, our representation office in Cairo handles company formation before GAFI and follows up on business licensing with different ministries like Petroleum, Industrial Development, and Tourism.

International Arbitration & Private Law

We provide premier legal representation in commercial arbitration disputes before the Cairo Regional Centre (CRCICA), drafting complex arbitration clauses, and advising on private international law.

Foreigners & Expat Egyptians

We document foreign marriages at the Ministry of Justice in Cairo, certify official documents at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, process expat residence permits at Abbassia Immigration Department, and manage inheritance assets for Egyptian expats.

Litigation & Criminal Defense

Our representation extends to the Court of Cassation, Supreme Constitutional Court, and Supreme Administrative Court in Cairo. We defend clients in economic crimes, public funds, cybercrimes, and before Cairo courts.
